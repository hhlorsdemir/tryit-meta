## Beta App Description

TryIt dresses you in real products from online stores. Take one full-length
photo of yourself once. Then, on any product page in a store app or browser,
tap Share and send the link to TryIt. Add a top, a bottom and shoes, and TryIt
shows the complete outfit on your own photo, not on a stock model.

How it works: Meta Muse Spark reads the product page and photos and writes a
short product card (which piece it is, its cut and length), so the right
garment is picked even from photos where the model wears a full outfit. Meta
Muse Image then dresses your photo in the selected pieces while keeping your
face, body, pose and background.

This build is the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon version: all features are
free and unlimited during judging, no account or purchase is needed.

## What to Test

1. Add your photo: full-length, facing the camera, arms at your sides, in
   everyday clothes (for example a t-shirt and trousers), good light.
2. Open a product in a store app or website (for example Trendyol,
   Hepsiburada or Koton), tap Share and choose TryIt. You can also paste a
   product link inside the app.
3. Add up to three pieces (top, bottom, shoes) and tap the button to create
   your outfit. It takes about 15 to 30 seconds.
4. Check that each piece matches its product (colour, details, length, fit)
   and that you still look like yourself.
5. Save or share the result.

Known limits: some stores block automated access (for example Zara); if a link
cannot be read, try another store. Photos in underwear or swimwear with only a
top selected may be refused by the image model's safety filter; use a photo in
everyday clothes or add a bottom piece.
